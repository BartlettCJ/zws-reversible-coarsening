\section{Scope and evidence status}
This Supplement documents the frozen model, the provenance of the selected rule, exact audits, confirmatory measurements, mechanism constraints, and the failure of the later v0.3.2 history-erasure endpoint. It was rebuilt from compact stored evidence without new simulation. The original v0.3.2 report and its figures are preserved as immutable historical records; the additive erratum and corrected derivative govern current interpretation.

Four evidence classes are kept distinct:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Discovery:} a tiny smoke search and its exploratory winner run.
\item \textbf{Confirmation:} frozen-rule repeats and the large exact reversal.
\item \textbf{Mechanism constraints:} density, finite-size, geometry, arrangement, matched-sibling, and ablation comparisons.
\item \textbf{Classifier diagnostics:} v0.3.2 results that diagnose a failed endpoint rather than answer the intended causal question.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Automaton definition}
\subsection{Alphabet and observables}
The four cell states are empty ($0$), positive excitation ($+$), negative excitation ($-$), and bound-neutral excitation ($\mathrm b$). For a lattice state $x$,
\begin{align}
Q(x)&=N_{+}(x)-N_{-}(x),\\
M(x)&=N_{+}(x)+N_{-}(x)+N_{\mathrm b}(x).
\end{align}
$Q$ is a signed additive charge. $M$ counts non-empty cells and is called occupancy or material in the software. The selected rule conserves $Q$ in every local block update but not $M$. All principal worlds were initialized in the global $Q=0$ sector. The phrase ``$Q=0$ sector'' must not be read as ``the rule conserves the value zero only'', ``$Q$ and $M$ both vanish'', or ``the world is empty''.

\subsection{Block code and phase schedule}
On a periodic $H\times W$ lattice with even dimensions, alternating $2\times2$ Margolus partitions cover the lattice. Each four-cell block is packed in base four to an integer in $[0,255]$. A permutation $f$ of those 256 codes supplies the forward update; $f^{-1}$ supplies the inverse. The checkerboard phase is part of the dynamical state. Exact reversal applies the inverse table while stepping phases in reverse order.

The frozen table is stored in \path{extensions/reversible_null_phase/rules/winner_d1fe99.json}. SHA-256 of the raw 256 unsigned-byte table, not the JSON serialization, is
\begin{center}
\code{d1fe99b5735d8e9be032347de2727dc5c52a7848df200ca26f67f28c8c1bc41c}.
\end{center}
The rule is a permutation; all 256 outputs are unique. Exhaustive enumeration verifies $Q(f(b))=Q(b)$ for every block state $b$. The same enumeration finds block states for which $M(f(b))\ne M(b)$.

The block code uses base-four order top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right,
\begin{equation}
c=s_{\mathrm{TL}}+4s_{\mathrm{TR}}+16s_{\mathrm{BL}}+64s_{\mathrm{BR}}.
\end{equation}
The construction starts from clockwise block rotation and exchanges 12 reversible output pairs; consequently, 24 of 256 inputs differ from the rotation scaffold. Two entries used in the main-text worked diagram are exact table values. Entry $f(1)=4$ maps $(+,0;0,0)$ to $(0,+;0,0)$ and preserves $(Q,M)=(+1,1)$. Entry $f(130)=154$ maps $(-,0;0,-)$ to $(-,-;+,-)$ and changes $(Q,M)$ from $(-2,2)$ to $(-2,4)$: one positive and one negative excitation are added as a balanced pair. The inverse permutation maps code 154 back to code 130. These examples illustrate transport and reaction channels; they are not a claim that either lookup alone is sufficient for the macroscopic behaviour.

\subsection{What was searched}
The historical discovery metadata records construction family \code{reactive\_rotation}, elite source \code{g0000\_reactive12}, final rank zero, 12 swaps, 14 unique rules evaluated, and 16 replicate rule evaluations. The search run was a smoke-scale campaign. It was not a representative sample of the enormous permutation space (metadata reports a theoretical base-10 rule-space logarithm of approximately 317.961), and the selected rule must be described as search-selected.

\section{Exact reversal and composition}
The large audit used a $4096\times4096$ periodic lattice (16,777,216 cells) with a populated central random patch, not a whole-world homogeneous start. The square patch spans 45\% of each dimension; its target internal proportions are 12\% positive, 12\% negative and 8\% bound-neutral, and the exterior is empty. After 65,536 forward block phases, the run reversed the phase schedule and applied the inverse permutation. The final recovered state was identical to the initial state at every cell. Global signed charge remained $Q=0$. Occupancy $M$ changed during the trajectory, as permitted by the rule.

The four $4096\times4096$ localized-patch confirmation runs supplied the reported final dense-interior median occupancy 0.572989, positive fraction 0.192542, negative fraction 0.192535, and bound-neutral fraction 0.187912. Normalized four-state entropy was 0.946343. Those four localized-patch confirmatory winner runs reached transient domain-scale maxima of 72.66--136.11 times their initial scale (median 98.4459); the final stored scales were 15.77--16.33 times initial (median 16.1118). This nonmonotonicity is why the paper says ``coarsening-like organisation'' rather than monotonic phase ordering.

\section{Endpoint definitions and initial-condition provenance}
\subsection{Distinct initial-condition families}
The large reversal and the four $4096^2$ growth/composition confirmations use the localized-patch family above. Whole-torus homogeneous-random studies are separate: the principal density map and later first-passage study use $1024^2$ lattices, with smaller $512^2$ comparisons and limited $2048^2$ checks in the recorded campaigns. In the homogeneous density family, an exact inventory with occupied proportions $3/8$ positive, $3/8$ negative and $2/8$ bound-neutral is shuffled over the entire torus. For target occupancy $\rho$ and $N$ sites, the documented rounding is $u=\lfloor\rho N/8+1/2\rfloor$, giving counts $(N-8u,3u,3u,2u)$. These ensemble constructions must not be pooled as the same initial condition.

\subsection{The implemented v0.3/v0.3.1 endpoint}
Let $a_i(t_j)$ be the areas of finite (non-wrapping) empty components returned by the frozen campaign's four-neighbour toroidal component code. The characteristic scale is the equivalent-circle radius of the area-weighted component area,
\begin{equation}
 L_j=\sqrt{\frac{\sum_i a_i(t_j)^2}{\pi\sum_i a_i(t_j)}}.
\end{equation}
When no finite empty component exists, $L_j=0$. ``Finite'' here excludes winding components; it does not mean that other components contain infinitely many sites on the finite torus. Historical topology implementations and the later topology correction remain versioned; this description does not retrospectively recompute any campaign.

Set $D=\max(L_0,1)$ and $r_j=L_j/D$. For a prefix through sample $k$, choose $j^*=\min\arg\max_{0\leq j\leq k}r_j$ and let $f_j$ be the largest finite empty component's fraction of all sites. The exact implemented predicate is
\begin{equation}
 C_k=\mathbf{1}[r_{j^*}\geq4\ \wedge\ r_k\geq2\ \wedge\ f_{j^*}\geq0.001
 \ \wedge\ (\forall j\leq k: Q_j=Q_0)].
\end{equation}
At least two snapshots are required. The largest-component fraction is evaluated at the \emph{first peak-scale sample}, not necessarily at its own separate maximum. No Fourier, low-$k$, entropy or mutual-information threshold enters $C_k$. Those diagnostics are recorded independently. The paper therefore does not describe the endpoint as a joint spectral-and-geometrical test.

For v0.3.1, sampled phases are spaced by 2,048. $T_{\rm hit}$ is the earliest sample with $C_k=1$. $T_{\rm persistent}$ is the earliest start of three consecutive true prefix flags, which can follow an earlier isolated hit. No event time is interpolated between observations; an absent event is right-censored at the trajectory's actual follow-up horizon. The implementation sources are \code{metrics.py:StrongDomainCriterion} and \code{nucleation.py:first\_passage\_from\_flags}, at the computational release identified in the provenance note.

\subsection{Earlier frozen endpoints are not identical}
The original 2026-08-25 confirmation required final coarse-density dispersion at least 8, at least twofold final empty-domain scale, a positive fitted late-half scale slope and exact charge preservation. Its strict sustained-coarsening result failed at the larger scale; the reproduced transient morphology was reported separately.

The later v0.1 homogeneous-density map used peak/final scale ratios of at least 4/2 and a peak largest finite-empty area of at least $\max(64,16\max(A_0,1))$, where $A_0$ is the initial largest finite-empty area, with $Q=0$ at every saved phase. Its \emph{separate} low-$k$ flag required radial shells $k=1,\ldots,4$ to have peak/final mean-power ratios of at least 4/2 and peak spectral-fraction ratio at least 2. The v0.3 family instead uses the 0.001 lattice-fraction condition above, as explicitly stated in the v0.3.1 preregistration. Thus the earlier approximately 27.5\% estimate is an operational result of that earlier campaign, not a directly interchangeable critical-density estimate. Within each unchanged endpoint, different time horizons can be compared without making this substitution.

\subsection{Different sources for the main image panels}
The main reversal figure uses localized discovery seed 260824 and an organised intermediate panel at phase 20,480. Its recovered panel displays the phase-zero state whose exact recovery was established by the 65,536-phase inverse audit. The main micro/macro figure instead uses untouched homogeneous $1024^2$ source \code{3cde465f2d2883dd5af4}, initial density 0.20, seed 6203 and phase 311,296. Its nested $64^2$ crop has counts $(1359,899,947,891)$ and normalized single-site entropy 0.987741. This image is distinct from the four localized confirmation runs' dense-interior statistic 0.946343. The v0.3.2 causal-endpoint failure does not invalidate a direct image of the untouched source.

\section{Arrangement and prepared-shape controls}
Prepared droplets and slabs retained or enlarged their imposed geometry but produced zero strong-domain endpoint crossings across 48 trials by 262,144 phases. Matched homogeneous starts produced 19 crossings in 24 trials. These are outcomes of a \emph{relative-growth} assay: the denominator is each trajectory's own initial finite-empty-domain scale. A prepared macroscopic shape is not on the same baseline as random soup. Accordingly, 0/48 is not evidence that seeded organisation failed to form or persist, and this contrast alone does not establish a basin barrier, history inhibition or failure of all compact seeds. The direct measurements of retained geometry remain valid, separately from the endpoint counts.

Earlier arrangement comparisons and phase controls are retained in the experiment reports. Their role is to constrain trivial initialization artifacts, not to establish a thermodynamic ensemble.

\section{Double conservation and reaction-pair ablations}
The exact-$Q{+}M$ sibling is a distinct permutation whose every local block update preserves both signed charge and occupancy. \section{Prevalence screen}
The generator screen counted 19 strong-domain rules among 256 independent charge-preserving candidates, a sample proportion of 0.074219 and Wilson 95\% confidence interval 0.048026--0.113001. This estimate pertains to the specified generator and protocol. It does not measure the fraction of all local permutations, all charge-preserving permutations, or all rule-construction families. The selected winner remains selected evidence.

\section{v0.3.2 classifier failure and correction}
\subsection{Intended intervention}
The v0.3.2 campaign branched deterministic trajectories at frozen source states. Global and hierarchical shuffles preserved each single-site state count, hence $Q$, $M$, and single-site entropy, and retained the partition phase. The intervention removed spatial arrangement while leaving the subsequent rule deterministic. Every one of 72 altered-branch intervention audits passed. The topology-fixed run is the only valid raw basis; the earlier topology-bug run is excluded.

The intended primary contrast was first passage to the existing strong-domain endpoint after shuffling versus an untouched continuation. This would have been informative only if the endpoint discriminated the organised source geometry from the shuffled geometry.

\subsection{Immediate state audit}
It did not. In Branch A primary global shuffles, mean low-wave-number power fell from 5,544.267654 to 43.719036: 99.211455\% of the source value was removed. Interface density rose from 0.364832 to 0.455946, anisotropy fell from 0.549951 to 0.159870, and nearest-neighbour mutual information fell from 0.0208694 to approximately $4.05\times10^{-6}$. The intervention therefore did erase measured organisation.

In all 20 Branch-B global shuffles, mean low-wave-number power fell from 7,246.310873 to 45.649658. Yet 9 of the 16 primary Branch-B replicates were assigned $R_{\mathrm{hit}}=0$, 14/16 hit by 4,096 phases, 15/16 by 6,144 phases, and the remaining row hit at 20,480 phases. The post-shuffle long-wave mean was only 729.492 at 8,192 phases and 1,127.719 at 16,384 phases, still far below the natural source mean. The classifier was therefore responding before long-wave structure had recovered.

\subsection{What is withdrawn and what remains usable}
Withdrawn: claims that the shuffled branches showed immediate recovery of genuine macroscopic organisation; that hidden multiscale precursor memory was thereby necessary; that erasing history inhibited or accelerated the transition; or that the result established a causal spatial-memory mechanism.

\begingroup\sloppy Retained: exact intervention preservation audits; direct evidence that shuffling erased long-wave structure, local mutual information, and anisotropy; deterministic post-intervention trajectories; recovery curves as descriptive dynamics; and the methodological lesson that a connected-cluster endpoint must be revalidated under the intervention's null distribution. The connected-empty-cluster explanation is a diagnostic supported by the observed state geometry, not a proof of equilibrium percolation.\par\endgroup

\section{Prior-art positioning}
Reversible block automata and conservative logic are foundational precedents \citep{FredkinToffoli1982,Margolus1984,ToffoliMargolus1987}. Lattice gases provide exact local collision and transport frameworks \citep{HPP1973,FrischHasslacherPomeau1986,RothmanZaleski1994,BoonEtAl1996}, and reversible lattice-gas work directly addresses macroscopic irreversibility under microscopically reversible dynamics \citep{Broadwell2001}. Q2R models provide reversible conservative statistical dynamics \citep{Takesue1987,UrbinaRica2016,Urbina2020}; reversible aggregation provides an especially close visual and mechanistic neighbour \citep{DSouzaMargolus1999}. Reversible number-conserving automata and partitioned constructions further narrow the architectural context \citep{BoccaraFuks2002,MoritaImai2001}. Two-species reversible automata with vacancies, reactions and multiple local charges are close modern neighbours \citep{MedenjakPopkovProsen2019,KlobasProsen2022}; reactive lattice-gas waves and Turing patterns provide a nonreversible pattern-forming comparison \citep{KapralLawniczakMasiar1991}. The full query log, DOI audit, comparison matrix, v0.2.3 correction audit, and bounded stopping rule are in \code{paper/prior\_art}.

The residual contribution is the combination, not any isolated ingredient: an exact four-state signed invariant with variable occupancy; delayed distributed coarsening-like organisation; exact large-lattice reversal; density and waiting-time characterisation; and a hierarchy of geometry, double-conservation, and reaction controls. The review does not claim exhaustive priority.
