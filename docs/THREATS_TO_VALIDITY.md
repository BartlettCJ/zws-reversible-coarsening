# Threats to Validity

## Construct validity

The model operationalizes “signed interaction” as a four-state exact-charge reversible CA. Literal graph splitting, coagulation, ancestry networks, repair, heredity, and open-ended evolution are not fully represented.

Mutual information, entropy, repeated patches, and perturbation spread are proxies. None is equivalent to life, organisation, irreducibility, or conscious character.

## Search bias

The optimizer can exploit its score. Protections include:

- separate components;
- simple and random controls;
- holdout seeds;
- novelty pressure;
- a gate against identity/rotation;
- disclosure of rule distance and searched volume.

Remaining risk: a rule may satisfy numerical bands in an uninteresting way.

## Finite-size effects

Toroidal boundaries can create collisions and recurrences unavailable in an effectively unbounded system. Repeat analyses across lattice sizes before claiming scale robustness.

## Reversibility restriction

Reversibility eliminates dissipative attractors and guarantees finite-state cycles. This is scientifically useful for predecessor tests but excludes many ordinary life-like automata. A later irreversible conserving family should be compared.

## Scheduling and partition dependence

Margolus partitions supply a specific local clock and geometry. Exact even translations are tested, but odd translations change partition phase. Results do not establish emergent spacetime without a master update schedule.

## Measure dependence

Finite-window ratios may depend on shape, center, temporal order, coarse-graining, process identity, overlap, and reference class. The package tests only a subset and explicitly retains failures.

## Postselection

Top rules are selected after many evaluations. Holdout analysis limits but does not eliminate winner’s curse. Confirmatory runs should freeze a rule and protocol before generating additional seeds.

## Software and hardware

GPU integer operations are checked against CPU when CUDA is available. Eshkol independently validates the local table, but the full simulator is not independently reimplemented there in version 0.1. Compiler or library defects remain possible.

## Metaphysical underdetermination

Every finite output is compatible with multiple metaphysical interpretations. The experiment tests formal descendants of the paper, not the actuality postulate itself.
