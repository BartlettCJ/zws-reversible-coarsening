# Interpretation Discipline

## A strong positive result would say

Within a declared finite rule family, local reversible rules can preserve an exact signed total of zero while producing nontrivial conversions, persistent correlations, bounded perturbation response, and perhaps recurring motifs. Some candidate finite-window conclusions may survive declared harmless redescriptions and exhaustion orders. Exact additive coarse-graining can compose across levels.

That would improve the paper in three ways:

1. the signed-nullity idea would have a concrete dynamics rather than only a paired-state construction;
2. “natural invariant” discussion would gain an explicit example of a locally motivated invariant with nearby nonzero sectors;
3. the process-measure and parthood sections would gain real counterexamples and success cases.

## It would not say

- that absolute non-being became something;
- that the actual cosmos has zero total energy;
- that the model is the base;
- that a stored trace differs metaphysically from its execution;
- that the simulation has primitive occurrence rather than ordinary physical implementation;
- that any motif is conscious;
- that an invariant causes organisation;
- that a unique observer/process measure exists.

## A negative result would say

Under the declared seeds, lattice sizes, budgets, rule representation, and search objective, the experiment did not locate a rule meeting the stated behavioural criteria. It may additionally show that candidate measures fail redescription or window robustness, or that selected coarse vocabularies do not close dynamically.

It would not show that no signed conservative dynamics can support life-like organisation. The rule space is much larger than the searched family, and literal graph splitting/merging remains untested.

## Wording suitable for a paper supplement

Use formulations such as:

> We constructed a finite reversible cellular-automaton family in which exact signed charge is preserved by every local 2×2 update. The search therefore tests dynamical richness within the zero fibre rather than repairing nullity globally. The reported rules are formal witnesses only; no claim about physical instantiation, occurrence, or consciousness follows.

Avoid:

> The simulation proves the universe emerges from zero.

> We created life from nothing.

> The cycle proves a beginningless base.

> The animation demonstrates consciousness.
